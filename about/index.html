About

I am a Master 2 student in applied mathematics at the University of Antananarivo, with a background in computer science and security before the mathematics took over.

Most of what I do sits between a proof and a program. Right now that means astronomical data — star formation in the interacting pair Arp 261 — alongside coding theory, numerical methods, and the parts of machine learning that are really just optimisation with better marketing.

I also co-founded Akademika, where I lead the technical side, and I tutor Python and mathematics. Teaching something is still the fastest way I know to find the holes in my own understanding, which is roughly why this site exists.

Outside that: chess, guitar, speed typing, and capture-the-flag competitions. I write in Malagasy, French and English.

Interests

  • Research — star formation, observational data reduction
  • Mathematics — numerical analysis, interpolation, optimisation
  • Coding theory — Reed–Muller and Reed–Solomon codes
  • Security — lattice and number-theoretic attacks on RSA
  • Quantum — stabiliser codes, error correction
  • Quant finance — alpha research, backtesting
