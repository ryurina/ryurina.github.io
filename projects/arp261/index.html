Arp 261

Mapping star formation across an interacting galaxy pair, from raw frames to region-by-region rates.

  • Astronomy
  • Python
  • Astropy
Ongoing

Question

Where does star formation occur in the interacting pair Arp 261, and is its spatial distribution consistent with a tidally-driven picture?

Background

Galaxy interactions compress gas and are widely associated with enhanced star formation. Arp 261 is a nearby pair with visible tidal structure, which makes it a reasonable case for asking not whether star formation is enhanced but where it sits relative to the interaction features.

Method

Reduction and analysis in Python, using Astropy and photutils.

  1. Calibration frames: bias, dark, flat.
  2. Astrometric solution and alignment across filters.
  3. Continuum subtraction to isolate narrowband line emission.
  4. Source detection and aperture photometry over candidate regions.
  5. Conversion of line luminosity to a star formation rate.

Data

Observational imaging in broadband and narrowband filters. Details of the instrument, exposures and observing conditions will be listed here once the reduction is finalised.
